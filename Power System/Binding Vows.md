“Binding Vows are the cornerstone of Jujutsu” - idk Sukuna probably

At the center of Jujutsu is the principle of sacrifice. Nothing is gained without the sacrifice of something. This is the key rule behind the phenomenon known as “**Binding Vows**”. In short, a **Binding Vow** is an exchange made between one or more parties involving Jujutsu. There are two types of **Binding Vows**:

1. One made between others
2. One made with yourself
    - This kind of **Binding Vow** is very complicated and encompasses many different forms
# Binding Vows With Others
Anyone can make a **Binding Vow** with another entity. In order to do so, one must lay out the terms of the agreement and spend 5 points of **Cursed Energy**. Each party involved must fulfill these steps in order to make the **Binding Vow**. Once the deal is struck, each party must fulfill the requirements laid out for them. If they do not do so, they will suffer consequences so terrible that even the King of Curses Ryomen Sukuna feared what would happen. 

There are some caveats. 

If a **Binding Vow** formed in this way is not given a time limit, it is theoretically possible to delay the fulfillment of the Vow indefinitely. In practice however, the intent behind each participant is measured and will cause the Vow to be broken if one participant or another intends to never fulfill their part. As a general rule, a **Binding Vow** between two parties should be given a time limit unless both parties trust each other completely. 

It is also a very esoteric piece of knowledge that **Binding Vows** between more than one participant only apply to the body of a sorcerer. Therefore, if a sorcerer were to move their soul into another vessel, then the **Binding Vows** they made with others would no longer be in effect.
# Self Imposed Binding Vows
**Binding Vows** imposed on oneself tend to be more complicated and nebulous than Vows made with others. The simplest way to describe them is by using Newton’s Laws of Conservation of Energy:

“Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed”

Self Imposed **Binding Vows** work similarly. Nothing is truly lost or gained, merely transformed into something else. Thus, a theoretical “Law of Conservation of Binding Vows” would be as follows:

“Binding Vows do not create or destroy Cursed Energy, only transform it”

This means we can think of anything involving Jujutsu as a “form” of **Cursed Energy**. Because Self Imposed **Binding Vows** only apply to the different “forms” of **Cursed Energy**, one cannot sacrifice anything they want, it has to involve CE or Jujutsu in some way. This also means that whatever you sacrifice must be of equivalent value to what you gain. Because of the Law of Binding Vow Conservation, you can't do things like:
- Sacrifice body parts to gain CE, Output, or Technique alterations
- Sacrifice esoteric concepts like intelligence
- Sacrifice physical attributes of your sorcerer like Strength, Speed, or Health

However, you can do things like:
- Sacrifice/Spend CE
- Alter/Reduce Output
- Change the parameters of your technique
    - Range
    - Damage
    - Target 
    - Duration
- Alter Barrier Rules

**Examples of Binding Vows**
- Nanami’s Overtime:
    - Sacrifice: can only use 80% power during the work day
	    - This is risky because it means Nanami cannot operate at full strength against dangerous opponents
    - Benefit: Once Nanami enters overtime, he can now operate at 120% power
- Revealing Ones Hand Binding Vow:
    - Sacrifice: explaining your technique
	    - This is risky because it informs your opponent on your fighting style and capabilities
    - Benefit: a boost in technique efficiency and power
- Hand Signs and Chants:
    - Sacrifice: time
	    - Risk: by using Hand Signs and Chants, a sorcerer opens themselves up to attack for a longer period of time
    - Benefit: greatly increased efficiency and output along with reduced cost.

Normally, you can break a **Binding Vow** with yourself without consequence. The only thing you would lose is whatever the **Binding Vow** gave you. However, it is possible to create a **Binding Vow** in such a way as to make it impossible to break. A great example of this is Miwa’s **Binding Vow** against Kenjaku
- Sacrifice: her ability to ever wield a sword again
    - This is insanely risky because Miwa relies on her sword skills in order to be useful as a combatant, so she is essentially neutering her ability to fight curses or curse users ever again
- Benefit: greatly increased power in a single strike

As such, sorcerers must be careful when constructing their Binding Vows so as not lock themselves into unnecessary penalties

Now, one might think that this would make Miwa pretty strong, but as seen in the Manga and Anime, sometimes your own weakness is too great to compensate for. As such, even with the added strength from her **Binding Vow**, Miwa was unable to even scratch Kenjaku, who is leagues beyond her in both strength and skill.

Because the heart of a **Binding Vow** is sacrifice, a weak sorcerer with nothing to sacrifice has very little to gain from **Binding Vows** as demonstrated by Miwa.

An important detail to note about **Binding Vows** is that they primarily involve a sacrifice that, “Brings the sorcerer closer to death”. This causes the sorcerers **Cursed Energy** to flare up in response. This flair of **Cursed Energy** is what allows for a **Binding Vow** to be made. So, when making a **Binding Vow**, remember that the greater the sacrifice—or the higher risk of death you undertake—then the stronger your **Binding Vow** will be.
### Binding Vows and Barrier Techniques
**Barrier Techniques** and **Binding Vows** go hand in hand. While the different types of **Barrier Techniques** will be explained later, an overview of how they interact with **Binding Vows** is located here.

The basics of **Barrier Techniques** are very simple to understand. They are barriers of **Cursed Energy** that can have rules imposed on them via **Binding Vows**. The most simple barrier is called a Curtain. It conceals everything within it from non-sorcerers as well as creates a night sky to lure out curses. The binding vow for creating a curtain can be thought of as such:
- Sacrifice: hand sign and chant
    - Risk: takes more time to deploy which means there is more opportunity for discovery. In addition, the hand sign means you cannot use hand sign related techniques at the same time you are deploying a curtain.
- Benefit: blocks out non-sorcerers as well as hides its interior from them. In addition, a night sky is formed to lure out curses.

Almost all **Barrier Techniques** are based on the Curtain in some way and use **Binding Vows** to alter their condition.

A great example is the Barrier that blocked Gojo from entering Jujutsu High. The binding vow is as follows:
- Sacrifice: anyone can enter the barrier
    - Risk: huuuuuuge risk because it leaves the caster vulnerable
- Benefit: Satoru Gojo is barred from entering

It is wise to note that Satoru Gojo is so strong, that in order to bar just him from entering, it was necessary to allow everyone else entry.

In addition, a Barrier can be strengthened by having the caster be located outside the Barrier itself. This sounds counter intuitive because a barrier should protect the caster. However, if a caster is trying to protect someone or something else, then the caster can make themself vulnerable in order to further protect their goal.
- Sacrifice: the caster is located outside the barrier
    - Risk: the caster is not protected by the barrier, therefore the barrier has increased odds of collapsing
- Benefit: the strength of the barrier is greatly increased

The more easily detected and vulnerable the caster is, the stronger the barrier will become.

Another great example is the barrier involved with **Domain Expansion**. Domain Barriers are insanely complicated because they create an interior subspace that overlaps and supersedes real geometry. This means that the interior of a Domain is completely customizable to the casters whim. In addition, a complex series of binding vows are woven into the barrier to make it function. 

Firstly, the **Sure-Hit Effect** is a result of imbuing the Domain Barrier with the **Lapse** of the technique. Secondly, a binding vow is made to strengthen the interior of the Domain Barrier. In exchange, the exterior is greatly weakened and made vulnerable to attack. Most sorcerers are ok with this sacrifice as only a fool would willingly enter someone else's domain. 

All of these binding vows, along with the **Hand Signs**, **Chants**, and high cost mean that **Domain Expansions** are essentially the Pinnacle of Jujutsu. It takes great skill in order to construct a Domain, and even higher amounts of skill to manipulate the **Binding Vows** of the Barrier. Even beyond that, manipulating the conditions on the fly is thought to be impossible for any except Satoru Gojo and Ryomen Sukuna.
# Mechanics
### Binding Vows With Others
1. Both parties lay out their terms
    - They do not necessarily need to be equivalent terms, as long as both parties agree
2. Each member must expend 5 Cursed Energy to seal the pact

There are no rules governing what happens when a binding vow between two or more people is broken. All that is needed is a warning: Not even Ryomen Sukuna was willing to break such a vow out of fear of the consequences
### Self-Imposed Binding Vows:
As you level up, you gain access to greater skill with **Binding Vows** as shown below.
- Level 10
    - Can make one Binding Vow
    - Cannot Make a Binding Vow during Combat
    - Can only manipulate up to 20% of your CE and Output
    - You can only manipulate the parameters of your basic lapse
    - You can manipulate the parameters of Simple Barriers
- Level 15
    - You can make binding vows during combat as a Special Action
    - This Special Action takes 2 actions
    - Can manipulate your lapse as well as one extension
- Level 20
    - You can have up to 3 active Binding Vows at once
    - You can manipulate up to 50% of your CE and Output
    - You can manipulate your lapse and all extension parameters
- Level 25
    - You can manipulate your reversal and Maximum Parameters
    - You can make Binding Vows as an Action
- Level 30
    - You can Manipulate 75% of your CE and Output
    - You can have unlimited Binding Vows
- Level 50
    - You can manipulate Domain Barriers
    - You can Manipulate 100% of CE, Output, and Technique Parameters
