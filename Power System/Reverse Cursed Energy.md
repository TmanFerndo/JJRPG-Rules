**Reverse Cursed Energy** (RCE) is a very nebulous concept. At its core, it is simply the opposite of **Cursed Energy**. Where **Cursed Energy** can be thought of as Negative (-), RCE can be thought of as Positive (+). However, RCE cannot be generated in the same way that CE is naturally generated from negative emotions. Instead, **Cursed Energy** itself is used in a mysterious process most closely described as “multiplying it against itself” to create **Reverse Cursed Energy**. Creating RCE is not something that can be taught or learned in a traditional sense and whether or not you are capable of creating it is largely dependent on talent and mental state. However, it is known that in times where a sorcerer is extremely close to death, the odds of understanding the RCE creation process increase.

One use of RCE is to flow it into your Cursed Technique. This causes your Cursed Technique to “flow in reverse” which produces an effect that is the direct opposite of your normal **Cursed Technique Lapse**. This is known as **Cursed Technique Reversal**.

The primary use of **Reverse Cursed Energy** is to fuel the **Reverse Cursed Technique** (RCT). RCT is a method of using **Reverse Cursed Energy** to heal the body. Normal negatively charged **Cursed Energy** cannot heal the body because of its negative properties, however Positive **Reverse Cursed Energy** can. The process of creating RCE converts two points of **Cursed Energy** into one point of RCE. As such, the process of using RCT to heal the body is twice as intensive and expensive as normal Cursed Energy Reinforcement (bracing).

For quick reference, the formula for generating RCE is as follows:
- RCE = ½ * X
	- Where X is the amount of CE invested (minimum of 2)
# RCT Healing/RCT Output
When healing via RCT, your Power Output is limited in a similar way to regular CE. However, because the conversion of CE to RCE is 2:1, RCT Output is conversely half as efficient as regular CE Output rounded down
- If your CE Output is 5 then your RCT Output would be 2

This means that anything affecting your CE Output will also affect your RCT Output.

When using RCT to heal, follow these steps:
1. Check Power Output. Your Output determines your maximum RCT multiplier 
    - For example, if your Output is x2, then the maximum multiplier would x2
    - If your Output is x5, then your maximum multiplier is x5
2. Your RCT multiplier starts at 0 and increases by 1 for every point of RCE spent (or every 2 points of CE).
    - You cannot exceed your maximum multiplier
3. Roll a d6 and multiply the really by 1% of your Max HP (rounded down).
4. Multiply the result by your RCT Multiplier

**RCT Formula**
- X * (1d6) * (1% Max HP)
    - Where X is ½ the amount of CE spent
# Reverse Cursed Energy Attacks/Healing Others
Not everyone who can use RCE can use it outside of their body. When you unlock RCT, roll a d100. On a result of 1-20, you are also able to use RCT on others. In addition, you can spend RCE on melee attacks. Humans do not take damage from RCE attacks but curses take 4x damage. When you spend RCE on an attack, you are capped by your RCT Output. When you go to heal someone else with RCT, there is a 50/50 chance that their body does not respond to the energy. If this is the case, then you cannot heal that person.
# Healing Severe Injuries
In this game, it is possible for your sorcerer to suffer severe injuries beyond surface level wounds. **Severe Injuries** can include severed limbs, broken bones or severe bleeding. These injuries require more effort to heal than standard RCT healing. These **Severe Injuries** will have a **Healing Threshold** associated with them. In order to heal these injuries the amount of health you would regain from RCT must meet or exceed the **Healing Threshold**. If you do not meet the **Healing Threshold** then the injury is not healed and no health is regained, however the RCE is still expended.
# Brain RCT
Healing the brain to reduce **Technique Burnout** after a **Domain Expansion** is a feat of absolute expertise seen only by Satoru Gojo and Ryomen Sukuna. One cannot use Brain RCT until they have met certain conditions as determined by the DM. It is a very risky and dangerous technique that requires the user to damage their brain and then heal it with RCT. This is an insanely risky process that very few individuals can even attempt.
- The **Healing Threshold** for Brain RCT is 90% of your Max HP
- When you perform Brain RCT, there is a chance you incur a Traumatic Brain Injury. Roll a d100, on a 76-95 you take 15% of your Max HP in damage. On a 96-100 you gain the **Traumatic Brain Injury Grievous Wound**.
- Every consecutive use of Brain RCT increases the likelihood of gaining the **Traumatic Brain Injury Grievous Wound** by 5%. This does not reduce the chance of taking damage which stays at 20%.
- After the 4th use of Brain RCT, **Domain Expansion** becomes unusable even if **Cursed Technique Burnout** is fully healed.
