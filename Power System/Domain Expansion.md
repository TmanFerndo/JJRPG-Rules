**Domain Expansions** are the pinnacle of Jujutsu Sorcery. They combine your Cursed Technique, Barrier Skill, and a myriad of Binding Vows to pull off. As such, a sorcerer capable of **Domain Expansion** is clearly a cut above the rest, capable of the pinnacle of Jujutsu feats. It costs 250 CE + 40% of your Maximum CE Pool to cast a domain, and once cast, a domain lasts until the caster dispels it or takes too much damage. In order to cast a domain, you need to create a two-handed sign and chant, “Domain Expansion: Name of Domain”. This takes your full turn and the Domain takes effect at the end of your turn. Your opponents will be able to take one full turn before your Sure-Hit activates, however, they cannot leave the range of your Domain Barrier. If an opponent chooses to cast their own Domain Expansion in response, a Domain Clash ensues. Regardless of your Domains sure-hit effect or function, all domains give the following benefits:
- 20% increase to CE Output
- All CE related abilities (Attacking, Bracing, Technique abilities) are reduced to 1 CE but treated like maximum output

In addition to these benefits, fully completed Domains incorporate a Sure-Hit effect that is derived from the Sorcerers Cursed Technique. In lethal Domains, this Sure-Hit effect is the primary attack of the lapse with the following properties:
- Your sure-hit automatically hits all targets three times on your turn without any use of your actions
- Your sure-hit attacks cost no CE
- Your sure-hit attacks are treated like maximum output attacks

The sure-hit effects of non-lethal domains vary from technique to technique but typically force individuals caught within the domain to obey certain rules dictated by the technique.

Once your Domain ends you enter into Technique Burnout and cannot use your Cursed Technique or cast another Domain Expansion for 3 rounds.
# Domain Barrier
Your Domains barrier is strengthened from the inside so as to make it impossible to escape. However, as a trade off, the outside of your Domain is weakened. The exterior of a Domain's barrier has an amount of HP equal to half the MP invested into **Barrier Techniques**. When a barrier is reduced to ⅔ of its max HP a hole is opened for 1 turn that allows curses and sorcerers through. When a Domain's barrier is reduced to 0, it collapses. When you unlock the ability to alter Domain Barriers with Binding Vows, you can shrink or grow the range of your Barrier. For every meter you shrink your Domains Barrier, it gains an increase to its HP equal to 10% of its base HP. The opposite effect takes place for every meter you extend your domain’s range. You cannot shrink your domain’s barrier into a space too small to contain both you and your opponent unless you have been trapped within the Prison Realm. For open Barrier Domains, your Output increases/decreases by 5% for every meter you decrease/increase its range. Domain barriers naturally heal 10% of their HP at the start of the caster's turn.
# Taking Damage Inside Your Own Domain
While you are inside your own Domain, taking enough damage can cause it to collapse. After your Domain has been expanded uncontested or after you have won a Domain Clash, if you drop below 75% of your Max HP you lose concentration on your domain and it collapses.
# Domain Clash
When two domains are cast within one turn of each other, the barriers clash together. Domains can clash for a maximum of 3 turns. Whichever domain wins a clash is dictated by the following:
- Domain Refinement which is represented by Barrier Investment. The higher the amount of MP in Barrier investment, the more refined your Domain
- Damage taken. The more damaged a sorcerer is, the more likely they are to lose a domain clash

In order to determine the winner of a Domain Clash, Clash Points will be awarded based on Domain Refinement and Damage Taken. Whoever has the higher Clash Point total by the end of the Domain Clash wins and their domain will take full effect.

**Refinement**
For every 50 MP of difference between the two sorcerers’ Barrier Investment, award one Clash Point to the sorcerer with the highest Barrier Investment.
- If the difference between the two Sorcerers' Barrier Investment is greater than 250 MP, then the domain with higher Barrier Investment immediately wins the clash

**Damage Taken**
For every 10% of their Max HP a sorcerer takes in damage while in a Domain Clash, award 1 clash point to the sorcerer that dealt the damage.
### Perfectly Even
By the end of a Domain Clash, if your Clash Points are even with your opponents, then the individual with the higher Domain Refinement wins the clash. If you both have the same amount of Refinement, then the clash doesn't end. Instead, the first person to take 10% of their max HP in damage loses the clash. 

In addition, when two individuals with exactly the same amount of Refinement clash Domains, there is a small chance that one of them will open their domain slightly before the other. Roll a d100. On a 98-100, one of the sorcerers opens their domain slightly before the other. 

Flip a Coin to decide who opens their domain first. The sorcerer who opens their domain first is able to affect their opponent with their Sure-Hit for the equivalent of One Action before the Clash begins.
### Domain Clashes of 3 or More Domains
If a Domain Clash involves 3 or more domains within 250 MP of each other, then the Domains enter into an extremely fragile state. If any other entity comes into contact with the barrier before a winner is determined, then the clash immediately ends and all domains collapse. If the barriers do not collapse, then award clash points as follows:  
- The most refined domain gets 1 clash point for every 50 MP of difference between it and the least refined domain  
- The second most refined domain gets 1 clash point for every 50 MP of difference between it and the least refined domain  
- All other domains gain 1 clash point

Play then proceeds as normal following Domain Clash rules. However, if one Domain is at least 250 MP greater than the domain with the next highest refinement, then that domain wins.
# Domain Clashes With 1 or More Open Domains
In a standard Domain Clash, the Barriers of Closed Domains interfere with each other and completely disable both Sure-Hit effects. However, when one or both of the Domains is expanded without enclosing a barrier (this phenomenon is called an Open Domain) then there are no Barriers to clash. Instead, the Sure-Hit effects themselves clash with each other. While the base effect is the same as with Closed Domains, there is some nuance that is important to note.
1. Being “Targeted” by a Sure-Hit Effect and being “Affected” by a Sure-Hit Effect are not the same thing
    - This is because the Sure-Hit exists in two parts. First is the “Command” that tells the Domain to attack a target. The second is the actual Attack itself which takes the form of the users imbued Technique.
2. Wherever the Sure-Hits overlap, they cancel each other out

This ultimately means that if a Sorcerer can selectively choose targets within their domain rather than target everything indiscriminately, they can choose which targets within their Domain are protected from the opposing Sure-Hit by selectively deactivating it against specific targets.

This phenomena is enabled by the nature of Open Domains lacking a barrier so the Sure-Hit still exists, but it is normally cancelled out by the opposing Sure-Hit. This allows for experienced Sorcerers like Sukuna to allow Mahoraga to adapt to Unlimited Void by leaving Megumi unprotected by Sukuna's Sure-Hit and forcing him to bear the adaptation process. In addition, this means that Open Domains whose range extends beyond the barrier of a closed domain can target the external wall of the barrier with attacks.
