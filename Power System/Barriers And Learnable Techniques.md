While the strongest sorcerers have Innate Cursed Techniques, there are aspects of Jujutsu that can enhance a sorcerer's kit outside of raw talent or blessings. These are learnable Techniques which include: Simple Domain, Falling Blossom Emotion, Domain Amplification, and Hollow Wicker Basket. In order to learn these techniques, a sorcerer must pay the MP cost associated with the technique. In addition to paying the MP cost, some techniques have downtime associated with them. This means that you have to spend in-game time in order to learn the technique. Some learnable techniques have to be taught by someone, other techniques gain an MP discount from being taught. Witnessing a learnable technique in combat can help you understand it better. If you witness a Learnable Technique at least 5 times, then you gain a 25% discount on the MP cost. If you witness a Learnable Technique 15 times, then you can learn it without being taught in addition to a 50% MP discount. Listed below are the various Learnable Techniques, their mechanics will be explained in detail under the Learnable Techniques Tab

# Barrier Investment
Your progress towards learning **Domain Expansion** is represented by **Barrier Investment**. This also uses MP but is different from Learnable Techniques. Unlike Learnable Techniques where you can choose when to spend MP, you cannot choose when to invest MP into Barriers. Instead, as a reward for completing certain quests or fulfilling criteria, you may be given the option to invest MP into Barriers. The more MP you invest, the closer to **Domain Expansion** you get and the stronger domain related techniques like **Simple Domain** and **Domain Amplification** become as well.
