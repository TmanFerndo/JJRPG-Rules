In Jujutsu Kaisen, **Cursed Energy** (CE) permeates the world. Every human has some amount of **Cursed Energy** though most do not have enough to use. **Cursed Energy** is created from negative emotions. Fear, Hate, Disgust, and Depression all generate **Cursed Energy**. 

For most people, this **Cursed Energy** is harmlessly released and dissipates into the world. However, in places with strong negative ties like Cemeteries, Schools, and Hospital's, **Cursed Energy** will congregate into large pools until they congeal into Spirits made of which will be explained later. There exist some individuals with a larger amount of **Cursed Energy** than normal. This is not necessarily tied to any negative emotion but rather a specific talent for using Jujutsu to generate **Cursed Energy** from any scrap of negative emotion. These individuals are known as **Jujutsu Sorcerers**. 

While in the Jujutsu Kaisen anime and manga, how much **Cursed Energy** a sorcerer can make is largely tied to talent, all player sorcerers will start with a Cursed Energy Pool of 100. This number can go up based on certain quest rewards as well as player level up but it cannot be easily or intentionally altered by a player. In order to use Jujutsu or Cursed Techniques, a sorcerer must spend points of **Cursed Energy**. These points of **Cursed Energy** are expended throughout the day and regenerate after a long or short rest.
# Cursed Energy Output
No matter how much **Cursed Energy** a Sorcerer has in their CE Pool, the amount they can output is limited. Think about it like a water tank with a hole in it. Even if you have a 20 gallon tank, if the hole is only half an inch wide, then the tank will drain slowly. If the hole is a foot wide, the tank will drain quickly. In this example, **Output** is represented by the hole. The higher your **Output**, the stronger your attacks will be and the more powerful your Cursed Technique will be as well. However, this also means you run the risk of running out of **Cursed Energy** faster. As you get stronger as a Sorcerer, your **Cursed Energy Output** will increase. Other things like quest rewards and training can increase your **Output**. Other effects like running out of CE can also reduce your **Output** temporarily.
- Whenever you drop below 50% of your CE pool, your **Output** is reduced by 25% of your **Max Output**
- Whenever you drop below 25% of your HP, your **Output** is reduced by 25% of your **Max Output**
- If you suffer a **Grievous Injury** like a broken bone or severed limb, your **Output** is reduced by 10% of your **Maximum Output** rounded up

All effects stack
# Cursed Energy Reinforcement
**Cursed Energy** can be conducted throughout the body to make it stronger. This mechanic informs other mechanics like Attacking, Damage, and Bracing which are explained later. You can also use your CE to make your body stronger when you need to make an ability check or saving throw. 

Whenever you need to make any ability check to saving throw, you can spend **Cursed Energy** to increase the relevant stat for that roll. For every 12 CE you spend, your **Ability** increases by 1 point. Once you have made the **Ability Check** or **Saving Throw**, your **Ability Score** reverts to normal. You cannot increase an **Ability Score** in this way above 18. For example, if you have an 8 in Strength, then the maximum buff you can give yourself is a +10.

In order to use this mechanic to buff your attack rolls, braces, and dodges you must use special actions that are detailed later. The **Aim Action** applies to attack rolls, the **Brace Action** applies to Braces, and the **Dodge Action** applies to Dodges.

Cursed Energy Reinforcement can also be used to increase your SPD stat when you take the Move Action. When used in this way, your SPD stat returns to normal once you complete your Move Action.
