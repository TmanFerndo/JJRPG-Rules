# Stealth
Not all encounters need to be solved with a fight. Sometimes a sorcerer will encounter a threat that is above their paygrade. In those scenarios, it would be better to avoid a direct fight rather than to seek it out. Or, perhaps a sorcerer wants to maintain the element of surprise. When a sorcerer wants to remain unseen, they can choose to take the **Hide Action**.
### Hide
In order to take the **Hide Action**, a sorcerer must have at least **Standard Cover**. If you take the **Take Cover** action to increase the quality of your Cover to **Greater Cover**, then you gain +1 accuracy to ability checks made to **Hide**. If you are behind **Total Cover** meaning that there is no line of sight between you and another creature, then you are automatically Hidden from them.

When you take the **Hide Action**, you make a **SPD Check**. The result of your SPD Check becomes your **Hiding DC**. If anyone wants to try and perceive you while you are hidden they must make a **PER Check** against your **Hiding DC**. If they succeed, you are spotted and gain no benefits from being Hidden. If they fail, you are Hidden until you are spotted or until you reveal yourself. Anyone can make a **PER Check** at the beginning of their turn and as a Quick Action to search for Hidden Creatures.
### Hidden
While you are hidden, you have +1 Accuracy to Attack Rolls made against creatures that you are Hidden against. In addition, if you initiate Combat against creatures that you are Hidden against, you have +1 Accuracy to the Initiative Roll. In addition, attack rolls that require line of sight automatically miss. Attacks that do not require line of sight have +2 difficulty to the attack roll. If you take the **Move Action** while Hidden, anyone you are Hidden from can make a **PER Check** to locate you. Using the **Step Action** does not trigger a **PER Check**.
