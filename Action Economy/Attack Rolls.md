Whether or not an attack hits or misses is not guaranteed. Even if there is an extreme difference between you and your opponent, there are still environmental factors that can affect luck. To represent this “luck factor” you will roll a ten sided dice (d10) whenever you make an attack. You then add your Attack Modifier (discussed below) to the roll and compare the result to your opponent's Speed Class (discussed below). If the result is greater than or equal to your opponent's Speed Class, you hit and deal damage. If the result is lower, then you miss.

# Attack Modifier
Your Attack Modifier is the bonus that you add to your Attack Roll to determine whether an attack hits or misses. It represents your skill in combat. Your Attack Modifier provides a bonus equal to your Perception Score

Thus, a sorcerer with a +2 in perception would have a +2 Attack Modifier—or in other words—a +2 to hit.

# Speed Class
Your Speed Class is the number that your opponent has to roll in order to hit you. Your Speed Class is calculated as follows:

SC = 6 + SPD

Thus, a sorcerer with a +3 Speed would have a Speed Class of 9, which means your opponent would have to make an attack roll (post modifiers) of 9 or higher in order to hit you.
