# Movement
In JJRPG, distance is measured in meters. On a square or hex grid, one grid space is equal to 1 meter and a sorcerer takes up a single grid space. In order to calculate your sorcerers movement speed, follow the formula bellow:
- 2m + SPDm

When you take the Move Action, you can spend Cursed Energy as stated under the Cursed Energy Reinforcement Rules to increase your SPD stat. When done in this way, your SPD stat is temporarily increased until you complete your Move Action.
### Move Action
When you take the move action on your turn, you can move up to your maximum movement speed. You can take this action as many times as you have actions available. For every 5 CE spent on your Move Action, you can add 1 meter to your movement.
### Step Action
When you take the Step Action, you move 1 meter. This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks
### Difficult Terrain
Sometimes, the terrain you are traversing is rough or harder to move through. As such, this Difficult Terrain halves your Movement Speed when you move through it. In addition, your Speed Class is halved if you end your turn inside Difficult Terrain
### Trip Action
When you take the Trip Action, you try to push your opponent over. Make a contested STR check against your opponent. On a success, your Target becomes Prone.
### Prone
The Prone Condition is applied whenever a sorcerer is lying flat on the ground, whether on their back or on their stomach. When prone, Ranged Attacks made against the prone creature have 2 points of difficulty applied to them and Melee Attacks have 2 points of accuracy. In addition, a prone creature can only take the Step Action to move. In order to stand up, a prone creature must spend 1 Action.
### Shove
When you take the Shove Action, you try to push your opponent away from you. Make a contested STR check against your opponent. On a success your opponent is pushed a number of meters away from you equal to 1 + your STR score (rounded down).

**Forced Movement:** If an effect, action, or Technique causes a creature to move against their will then the creature does not suffer from or trigger effects, abilities, actions, or techniques that trigger when a creature moves unless otherwise stated.
### Take Cover
The Take Cover Action allows a Sorcerer to duck behind cover. When you use the Take Cover Action, the cover you are currently in is increased by one level. You cannot Take Cover if you have already taken the Dodge Action and vice versa.
### Falling
Some technique effects or player actions can cause a sorcerer to be in a position where they will fall. Unless you have some method of aerial movement, if you start your turn in the air you begin to fall at a rate of 10m/turn times the number of consecutive turns spent falling up to a maximum of 50m/turn. At the beginning of your turn, before you can take any Actions or Quick Actions, you fall at the current rate. When you hit the ground, you take damage:
- 10(1d10 + 5) damage
    - +10x for every 10m you fall
### Jumping
You can jump a vertical distance equal to half your movement speed + half your STR score. You can horizontally jump a distance equal to your movement speed plus half your STR score. Jumping is treated like taking the Move Action and can be enhanced in the same way.
