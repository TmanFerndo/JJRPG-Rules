***
After you have hit an attack, you deal damage. The damage that you deal is based on a few things:
- Your Strength
- Your Output
- CE spent
- Weapon used
  
When determining damage, follow these steps:
1. Check your Strength Score. The type of dice you roll is determined by your Strength
    - (-6)-6 = d4
    - 7-9 = d6
    - 10-12 = d8
    - 13-15 = d10
    - 16-18 = d12
2. Then, add a Bonus to damage equal to half your strength score.
3. Then, check your Power Output. Your Output determines your maximum damage multiplier
4. Spend Cursed Energy. Your damage multiplier starts at 1x and increases by 1 for every point of Cursed Energy you spend.
	- In order to deal damage to a curse, your attack MUST have at least 1 point of CE invested.
5. Multiply the damage by your multiplier
### Example
Judas has a Strength of 6. This means his base damage is: 1d4 + 3. Judas’ Output is 25x which means his maximum multiplier for damage is 25x. If he spends 25 Cursed Energy on a melee attack, then Judas can deal 25(1d4 + 3) damage.
# Black Flash
Whenever you make an attack roll that hits, roll a d100. On a 1-99, the attack proceeds as normal. On a 100, the attack is a **Black Flash**. This has a variety of effects:
- Your output is increased to **120%** of its current value for the next 3 rounds
- The attacks damage is multiplied by **2.5**
- For the next 3 rounds you go into a state of extreme focus comparable to “flow state” or “the zone”. Therefore, you hit subsequent **Black Flashes** on 80-100 while in this state.
- After you have left “the zone” your understanding of Cursed Energy is permanently altered. Therefore, subsequent attacks have a higher chance of being a **Black Flash**. You now only need to roll a 95 or above on a d100 to land a **Black Flash**. In addition, your output is permanently increased by **+1x** and your Maximum CE Pool is increased by **20 points** which represents your better understanding of Cursed Energy
    - This “Deeper Understanding” effect does not stack with subsequent black flashes after the first
# Severe Injuries
Amidst the heat of battle, injuries are likely to occur. Most are inconsequential but every now and again a sorcerer can become so injured that it becomes a problem even for them. A sorcerer can gain a **Severe Injury** in many ways:
1. Taking more than a **⅙** of their max health in damage in one attack
2. Taking more than **¼** of their max health in damage in one attack
3. Being hit by **special technique moves**

Depending on how an injury is incurred it will have varying negative effects. There are two different classification of **Severe Injury** that indicate the severity:
- **Minor Wounds** are mostly inflicted by receiving **⅙** of your health or more damage in a single attack and have small effects. They are easy to heal and their debuffs are minor.
- **Grievous Wounds** are primarily inflicted by receiving **¼** or more of your health in damage in a single attack and have major effects. They are very difficult to heal and their debuffs can be fight altering.

When you receive a **Severe Injury**, your **Maximum HP** is temporarily reduced by an amount equal to **half the damage dealt** to inflict it until the injury is healed, either naturally or via **RCT**. In order to heal an Injury with **RCT**, you must heal enough damage in a single use of to meet or exceed the **Healing Threshold** of the injury. In addition, when healing a wound with **RCT**, you only regain health if you exceed the **Healing Threshold** of the injury. Health regained in this way is equal to the difference between the amount rolled for **RCT** and the **Healing Threshold**. For more information on **RCT Healing**, see the sections above.

### Special Wounds
**Cursed Technique Burnout:**
- after your Domain Expansion ends, your Cursed Technique becomes “overheated” and you gain the wound Cursed Technique Burnout. This lasts for 3 rounds, during which you cannot use your Cursed Technique or Expand your Domain again. A sorcerer who has not unlocked the secret of Brain RCT cannot heal this wound before its time is up. In addition, a Sorcerer cannot heal Cursed Technique Burnout while they are at less than 80% HP or have injuries that are not stacks of External Bleeding.
	- HT: 90% of Max HP

**Soul Damage:**
- Some entities are capable of targeting the shape of your soul. Any attacker that is capable of “perceiving the contours of the soul” or that is welding a weapon that can directly contact the soul (such as The Soul Split Katana) can deal Soul Damage. If you are the target of Soul Damage and you do not possess an awareness of your soul, you will be unable to block Soul Damage via Brace. In addition, you will be unable to heal any wounds inflicted via Soul Damage with RCT unless you can perceive your own soul. Wounds inflicted with Soul Damage will heal naturally after the timer associated with them has passed or if appropriate items are applied. The primary exception is Mahito’s Idle Transfiguration Technique which cannot be healed from. This is because IT changes the shape of the soul directly instead of dealing damage to the soul. Any damage dealt via IT is permanent.
