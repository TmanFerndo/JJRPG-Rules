***
Many sorcerers choose to employ weapons to enhance their combat, though for many it becomes a crutch. The right sorcerer with the right weapon can become a demon on the battlefield, even without a cursed technique. Standard weapons give either a +3 or +6 to the wielders STR score when attacking depending on its type and all non-cursed tool weapons can only be imbued with a maximum of 5 Cursed Energy. If a weapon is imbued with more than 5 CE then it will break after the attack. In addition, Weapons can have Features attached to them which change how they function in combat. Some weapons require a certain amount of skill to use effectively. If you are not properly trained with a certain weapon, you may not be able to use all of its features and if the weapon is particularly difficult to use then you may suffer penalties to your attack rolls with it.

Instead of "damage types" all weapons and Technique Effects are given a **Damage Feature** that determines what kind of injuries they can inflict.
### Breaking a Weapon
When a weapon is overcharged with CE it will break. In addition, Cursed Tools can also break if put under enough strain. If you use your Reaction to Brace while wielding a weapon, compare the incoming damage to the amount blocked by your Brace. If 20 or more damage would get through your Brace, then there is a 5% cumulative chance that the weapon breaks for every 20 damage that is not blocked. If you attack with a weapon and your target uses their Reaction to Brace, compare your damage with the amount blocked by their Brace. For every 20 damage that is blocked there is a cumulative 5% chance the weapon breaks. Only roll to see if your weapon breaks after damage is dealt. Special Grade Cursed Tools cannot break.

### Weapon Mastery
Weapon Mastery is a measure of how skilled you are with a weapon. Whether it be hands and fists, swords, spears, or axes, your level of Mastery with your weapons can be improved. In order to gain Mastery in a weapon, you must spend 150 MP and 3 weeks of Down Time (or one week for every 50 MP spent). Once you have achieved Weapon Mastery you gain access to its **Mastery Features**. If a weapon has the **Skilled Feature**, you must first meet the requirements of the Skilled Feature before you can begin spending MP on Mastery.
# Fighting Styles
Once you have achieved **Mastery** with a weapon, you can begin working towards a **Fighting Style**. By investing 100 MP and two weeks of Downtime, you can choose to learn a fighting style that applies to your weapon. Each fighting style is associated with one or more weapons. You must have mastery in one or more those weapons in order to learn their associated fighting style.

# Cursed Tools
Cursed Tools are weapons and tools infused with Cursed Energy. As such, they can be used to attack Cursed Spirits. Some Cursed Tools are imbued with a power or even a Technique. Cursed Tools are more resistant to Cursed Energy so any Cursed Tool that takes the form of a Weapon is not bound by the 5 CE limit.

Cursed Tools are classified by Grade based on how powerful they are just like sorcerers and Cursed Spirits.
