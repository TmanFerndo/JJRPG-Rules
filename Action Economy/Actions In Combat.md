# Actions In Combat
When in combat sorcerers have limited time to react to stimuli. As such, the actions they can take are limited to what they can accomplish in brief moments of time. On their turn, a curse, sorcerer, or curse user can take up to **three Actions** on their turn as well as a **Quick Action**. Some Actions can only be taken once, and others can be taken more than once per turn. **Quick Actions** are actions that do not take up as much time as a regular Action. If an Action listed below has the **Quick Action Tag**, then it can be used as a Quick Action. Quick Actions can also be taken as a normal Action, however some actions can only be used once a turn. In addition to the actions you can take on your turn, every sorcerer has access to a **Reaction**. A Reaction is a once per round action that happens in response to someone else's actions. An Action can only be taken as a **Reaction** if it has the **Reaction Tag** and a Reaction cannot be used as a **Quick Action** or as an **Action**.

**Attack** - the sorcerer goes on the offensive, attempting to strike at their opponent
- Subject to **Multiple Attack Penalty**

**Prepare** - when you take the Prepare Action, you forgo using your Action on your turn to give yourself an extra Reaction

**Opportunity Attack** - in response to a target moving out of your reach, you can make an attack against that target.
- Reaction

**Grapple** - the sorcerer goes on the offensive, attempting to grab their opponent to lock them in place
- Actions with the **Grapple Tag** can only be activated after a successful Grapple  
- Subject to **Multiple Attack Penalty**

**Shove** - the sorcerer attempts to push a target away from them
- Subject to **Multiple Attack Penalty**

**Hide** - the sorcerer attempts to use the cover of the surrounding area to hide from enemies

**Trip** - the sorcerer goes on the offensive and attempts to knock an opponent over
- Subject to **Multiple Attack Penalty**

**Drag** - the sorcerer attempts to move the target of their Grapple
- Grapple

**Pin** - the sorcerer attempts to adjust their Grapple to pin their target
- Grapple
- Subject to **Multiple Attack Penalty**  

**Throw (Person)** - the sorcerer attempts to throw a pinned target
- Pin

**Injure** - the sorcerer attempts to heavily injure a Grappled Target
- Grapple
- Subject to **Multiple Attack Penalty**

**Disarm** - the sorcerer attempts to disarm their opponent
- Grapple
- Subject to **Multiple Attack Penalty**

**Draw/Stow** - the sorcerer takes out or puts away an item, weapon, or Cursed Tool in their possession.
- Quick Action

**Move** - the sorcerer repositions themself in combat

**Step** - the sorcerer repositions but is slower and more deliberate

**Manipulate** - the sorcerer takes a moment to interact with an object around them
- Quick Action

**Aim** - the sorcerer takes time to line up their next attack
- Action

**Dodge** - the sorcerer focuses on defense, attempting to dodge out of the way of incoming attacks
- Reaction

**Brace** - the sorcerer focuses on defense, attempting to block incoming attacks
- Reaction

**Technique** - the sorcerer uses their Cursed Energy to activate a part of their Technique
- **Multiple Attack Penalty** if eligible

**Chant** - the sorcerer takes time to chant certain words and phrases to enhance their technique

**Hand Sign** - the sorcerer takes time to create one or more Hand Signs to enhance their technique
- Quick Action

**RCT** - the user channels their CE to generate RCE to heal themselves.
- Quick Action

**Take Cover** - the sorcerer ducks behind a piece of terrain to hide from an attack 

**Special Action** - some actions (like technique abilities) may need to be specially classified by the DM.

More detailed descriptions of these actions are listed below.

### Aim
As an **Action**, you can aim your next attack. When you take the **Aim Action**, you can increase your **Perception** on your next attack. For every 12 CE spent on the **Aim Action**, you can increase your **Perception** by 1 point up to a maximum score of 18 on your next **Attack Action**. Multiple uses of the **Aim Action** do not stack, but instead affect separate **Attack Actions**.

In addition, when you take the **Aim Action**, you can use your next **Attack** to “call your shot”. This means you can target a specific part of your target to inflict an **injury**. If you hit and deal enough damage, you can inflict an **injury** on that part. You may not have direct control on the type of **injury**, but it will be an **injury** applicable to that part of the body.

### Brace
When an attack hits you (but before damage is rolled) you can use your **Reaction** to **Brace**. When you do this, you spend CE to reduce the damage from an incoming attack. The amount of damage that you can reduce is determined the same way as attack damage but using **Durability** instead of **Strength**. A single **Brace** can only guard against one attack, and multiple uses of this **Reaction** are required to guard against multiple attacks. In addition, the amount of CE you spend on a **Brace** is not limited by your **Output** like it is when you make an attack.

### Chant
Chanting is often a requirement for certain Jujutsu Techniques but it can also be used to enhance your technique. If your technique requires Chanting in order to activate, it will tell you how many uses of the **Chant Action** are required to activate your Technique. Once you have learned how to no longer include chants for the activation of your technique, you can use the Chant Action to enhance the power of your technique.

### Dodge
When you are targeted by an attack (but before the Attack Roll is made) you can use your Reaction to take the Dodge Action. When you take the Dodge Action, you can increase your Speed against one incoming attack. For every 12 CE spent on the Dodge Action, (capped by your Output) you can increase your Speed stat by 1 point against one incoming attack up to a maximum score of 18. Multiple uses of this action do not stack. Instead, they guard against separate attacks. You cannot take the Dodge Action if you have already taken the Take Cover Action.

### Hand Sign
Hand Signs can be made as a Quick Action. Just like Chants, your Technique may require a specific amount of Hand Signs to be made for Technique Activation. Once you have learned to subtract Hand signs from the Activation Requirements only your Technique, you can add them back in to boost your technique.

You cannot gain this boost from Chants or Hand Signs for parts of your Technique that do not use Hand Signs or Chants or that use different Hand signs or Chants. For example, Gojo’s Hollow Purple chants do not affect his Output on Lapse: Blue.

### Hide
In order to take the Hide Action, a sorcerer must have at least Standard Cover. If you take the Take Cover action to increase the quality of your Cover to Greater, then you have a +3 bonus to ability checks made to Hide. If you are behind Total Cover or if the line of sight from you to the creatures you are trying to hide from is blocked, you are automatically Hidden.

When you take the Hide Action, you make a SPD Check. The result of your SPD Check becomes your Hiding DC. If anyone wants to try and perceive you while you are hidden they must make a PER Check against your Hiding DC. If they succeed, you are spotted and gain no benefits from being Hidden. If they fail, you are Hidden until you are spotted or until you reveal yourself. Anyone can make a PER Check at the beginning of their turn and as a Quick Action to search for Hidden Creatures.

### Move
When you take the move action on your turn, you can move up to your maximum movement speed. You can take this action as many times as you have actions available. For every 5 CE spent on your Move Action, you can add 1 meter to your movement.

### Opportunity Attack
An opportunity attack is a special Reaction that can be taken to try and hit an enemy that is running away. Attacks of Opportunity are triggered by any non-forced movement that your enemy makes unless otherwise specified. Attacks of Opportunity must be Melee Attacks with a weapon you are holding.

### Prepare
When you take the Prepare Action, you forgo using your Action on your turn. In exchange, you gain an extra Reaction in addition to the one you already have. This Reaction lasts until the start of your next turn and can be used on any Reaction available to you.

### Shove
When you take the Shove Action, you try to push your opponent away from you. Make a contested STR check against your opponent. On a success your opponent is pushed a number of meters away from you equal to 1 + half your STR score (rounded down).

### Step
When you take the Step Action, you move 1 meter. This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks

### Take Cover
The Take Cover Action allows a Sorcerer to duck behind cover. When you use the Take Cover Action, the cover you are currently in is increased by one level. You cannot Take Cover if you have already taken the Dodge Action and vice versa.

### Technique Action
The Technique Action encompasses any action that activates an aspect of your technique. Standard applications of your lapse will likely take about 1 action, however some Techniques may take longer or shorter.

### Trip
When you take the Trip Action, you try to push your opponent over. Make a contested STR check against your opponent. On a success, your Target becomes Prone.

# Multiple Attack Penalty
In the Action List, there are some Actions with the Multiple Attack Penalty tag. This means that the action is subject to the Multiple Attack Penalty. The Multiple Attack Penalty applies a cumulative -2 penalty to the hit roll (or whatever the equivalent roll is) for every time you repeat an action. Say you take three attack actions on your turn:

1. 0 penalty
2. -2 penalty
3. -4 penalty

Some Technique Abilities may allow you to take extra actions on your turn. If this is the case, these extra actions are also subject to MAP unless otherwise stated.
