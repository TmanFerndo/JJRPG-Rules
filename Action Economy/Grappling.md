When you take the Grapple Action, you make an Attack Roll against a target. On a hit, the target becomes grappled. In order to Grapple you must have at least one hand free. Trying to Grapple with only one hand free results in a -2 to Attack Rolls and STR Checks made to Grapple your target. While grappled, the following effects are applied:
- Your target cannot move
- Your target has a -2 to attack rolls against you
- Neither you nor your target can use melee weapons that do not have the Grapple or Free-hand tag
- Neither you nor your target can use the Manipulate Action
- Neither you nor your target can take the Dodge Action

While you are Grappling a target, you gain a +2 to melee Attack Rolls against your target. In addition, you can attempt Actions with the Grapple tag. Your target can attempt to break out of your Grapple at the start of their turn and as an action. They make a contested STR check against you. On a success, they are freed from the Grapple. This is not subject to the Multiple Attack Penalty. In addition, your target can also attempt to grapple you. If they succeed, then neither of you gain an additional bonus to attack rolls against each other and all actions require a contested strength roll. At any time on your turn, you can choose to end a grapple for free. This means you lose the benefits of a grapple. However, if you are also grappled by your opponent, you cannot end the grapple.
### Drag
When you take the drag action, you attempt to move and force your target to move with you. When you take this action, make a contested STR check against your target. On a success you can move at half speed and your target is forced to move with you. On a failure, you do not move and your action is wasted.
### Pin
When you take the Pin action, you attempt to adjust your grapple in such a way as to completely immobilize your opponent, putting them at a severe disadvantage in the Grapple. When you attempt to Pin your opponent, make a contested STR check against your opponent. On a success, your opponent becomes Pinned. While Pinned, the Grapple effects are adjusted as follows:
- Your target has a -4 to attack rolls against you
- You gain a +4 to melee Attack Rolls against your target

A pinned target can attempt to break out of a Pin at the start of their turn and as an action. They make a contested STR check against you and on a success both the Pin and Grapple end.
### Injure
When you take the Injure Action, you attempt to heavily damage your opponent. Make an Attack Roll against your target. On a hit, you inflict a Minor Wound to an area of your choice instead of dealing damage. This wound must be of a type that your weapon can inflict.
### Disarm
When you take the Disarm Action, you attempt to force your opponent to drop their weapon. Make an opposed STR check against your target. On a success, they are forced to drop their weapon next to them.
### Throw
When you take the Throw Action, you attempt to throw your opponent away from you. Make an opposed STR check against your target. On a success you throw your opponent 6 + your STR score meters away from you and they take 1d6 +3 damage. On a success of 6 or more, your opponent is thrown 10 + your STR meters away and they take 2(1d6 + 6) damage.

When you take the throw action, you can choose to imbue an amount of Cursed Energy into it as if it were an attack. For every point of CE that you invest, the multiplier on the damage the throw would deal is increased by one. You must invest this CE before you make the contested check and you cannot exceed your Output.
