The New Shadow Style is a method of Jujutsu that incorporates [[Simple Domain]] and Sword Drawing Techniques (Iai) to enhance a sorcerer's combat capabilities. In order to learn New Shadow Style Techniques a Sorcerer must first learn [[Simple Domain]]. In addition, many New Shadow Style Techniques require or benefit from a weapon with the **Quick Draw** feature.
