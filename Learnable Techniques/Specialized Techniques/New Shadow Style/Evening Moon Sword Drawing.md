 - 750 MP
- 1 week of Downtime
- Must be taught
- Cost: 20 CE
- Upkeep: 5 CE

Evening Moon Sword Drawing is a more advanced form of [[Batto Sword Drawing]]. It allows the user to add additional effects into their [[Simple Domain]]. While Evening Moon Sword Drawing is active, your [[Simple Domain]] gives a 20% increase to your **Output** as well as applying a 20% decrease to the **Output** of techniques entering the [[Simple Domain]]. In addition, your attacks are not subject to the **Multiple Attack Penalty** while Evening Moon Sword Drawing is active. If you are hit by a technique or projectile entering your [[Simple Domain]], you can use your reaction to make a DC 9 PER check. On a success the attack misses instead.
