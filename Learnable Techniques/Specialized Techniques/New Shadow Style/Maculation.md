***
- 100 MP
- 1 week of Downtime
- Must be taught
- Cost: 10 CE
- Upkeep:5 CE

This technique is a more advanced form of [[Simple Domain]]. It creates a matrix of Cursed Energy that automatically intercepts targets in a similar manner to [[Evening Moon Sword Drawing]] and [[Falling Blossom Emotion]]. However, this technique also allows the user to automatically counter attack as well. When you cast this technique, you spend the rest of your turn taking the **Prepare Action** and any attacks that strike you are automatically blocked by **Brace** without spending a **Reaction**. In addition, if the attacker is within your [[Simple Domain]], you can automatically make an attack against them as a **Reaction**. In addition, because Maculation uses a matrix of Cursed Energy rather than a single barrier, the range of your [[Simple Domain]] is doubled while Maculation is active.
