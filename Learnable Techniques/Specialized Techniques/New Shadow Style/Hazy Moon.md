- 400 MP
- 1 week of Downtime
- Must be taught
- Cost: 10 CE
- Upkeep: 1 CE

This technique allows the user to shape their Cursed Energy into the form of a blade. This is often used to complete a broken blade so that a [[New Shadow Style]] user can remain relevant in a fight after their weapon is broken. This can also be used to summon weapons made of Cursed Energy.
