***
- 25 MP
- 1 week of Downtime
- Must be taught

Batto Sword Drawing is the most basic technique of the [New Shadow Style](./New%20Shadow%20Style.md). It focuses on pure offense and is the fastest technique in the [[New Shadow Style]]. If a target enters the range of your [[Simple Domain]] while it is active, you can use your **Reaction** to make a **Quick Draw** attack against the target. Any **Quick Draw** attacks you make will automatically move you towards a target within your [[Simple Domain]] if they are out of your melee attack range. In addition, any **Quick Draw** attacks you make with Batto Sword Drawing gain +1 Accuracy to the attack roll. In addition, your damage bonus is doubled.
