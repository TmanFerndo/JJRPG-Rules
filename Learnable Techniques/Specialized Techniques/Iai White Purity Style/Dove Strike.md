The most basic technique of the [[Iai White Purity Style]]. Similar to [[Batto Sword Drawing]], Dove Strike is achieved by the user pointing the hilt of the blade towards their opponent and imbuing the sheath with immense amounts of Cursed Energy until it causes an explosive reaction that sends the blade flying out at extreme speed. When you take the **Move Action**, you can choose to make a **Quick Draw** attack with a weapon that has the **Quick Draw** feature as part of the **Move Action**. When you make a **Quick Draw** attack in this way, your damage die uses your **Speed** score to determine die size and bonus. In addition, you must move in a straight line towards the target of your attack.
