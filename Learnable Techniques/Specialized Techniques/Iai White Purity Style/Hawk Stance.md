***
- 250 MP
- 1 week of downtime
- Must be taught
- Requires [[Dove Strike]]

This technique uses a unique stance that allows the user to better aim their blade. By treating their sword like a projectile, they are able to target weak points on their opponent and deal devastating blows. When you make a **Quick Draw** attack through [[Dove Strike]], you can use a **Quick Action** to take the **Aim Action** before you roll the attack. When you take the **Aim Action** in this way, your damage bonus is doubled on the **Quick Draw** attack that activated this feature.
