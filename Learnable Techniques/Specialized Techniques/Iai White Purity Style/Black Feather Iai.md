***
- 1000 MP
- 1 week of downtime
- Must be taught
- Requires [[Owl Guard]]

This technique is the pinnacle of the [[Iai White Purity Style]]. It uses a reverse grip hold on the blade to increase the efficiency of the blade's trajectory. Whenever you make a **Quick Draw** attack through [[Dove Strike]], you gain a bonus to your **Speed** Stat equal to half your **Speed** Stat. In addition, for every 4 meters that you move in a straight line as part of [[Dove Strike]], your **Maximum Output** on your attack increases by 5. Whenever you move using [[Dove Strike]], your movement does not provoke **Opportunity Attacks**.
