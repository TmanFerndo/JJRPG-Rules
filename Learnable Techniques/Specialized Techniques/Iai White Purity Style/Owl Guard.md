- 400 MP
- 1 week of downtime
- Must be taught
- Requires [[Hawk Stance]]
- Cost: 20 CE

A more advanced version of [[Dove Strike]] that pre-charges the blade with Cursed Energy. By pre-charging in this way, the user is able to react to incoming attacks with blinding speed in order to catch their opponent off guard. Whenever you stow a weapon that has the **Quick Draw** feature you can spend 20 CE to enter into a reactionary posture. This guard lasts until you take your next action or until after you are hit by an attack. When you are hit by an attack while Owl Guard is active, you can choose to spend a reaction to force a contested **Perception** Check between you and your attacker. If you succeed, then the attack misses instead and you can make a **Quick Draw** attack as if through [[Dove Strike]]. If you fail, the attack proceeds as normal and Owl Guard drops.
