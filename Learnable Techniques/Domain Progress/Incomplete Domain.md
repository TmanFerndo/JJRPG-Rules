***
An Incomplete Domain does not incorporate a **Barrier**. As such it is highly limited:
- The Domain must incorporate a physical barrier like walls in order to contain it
- The Domain does not have a **Sure-Hit Effect**

An Incomplete Domain may not incorporate a **Sure-Hit Effect**, but it does retain the other benefits of a Domain. Incomplete Domains are not as resource intensive as completed Domains. They only cost 125 CE + 20% of your Max CE to cast.
