Falling Blossom Emotion is an **Anti-Domain Technique** that is a closely guarded secret within the Big Three Sorcerer Clans. It requires the user to hold a specific stance. This technique completely shrouds the user in Cursed Energy that automatically intercepts and deflects attacks. It takes **1 Action** to activate Falling Blossom Emotion. At the start of each subsequent turn, you must choose whether or not you will maintain Falling Blossom Emotion. Deciding to maintain or drop Falling Blossom Emotion costs no actions. While Falling Blossom Emotion is active, you cannot take any **Actions**, **Quick Actions**, or **Reactions** except for the **RCT Action**. Whenever you are hit by a physical attack, Falling Blossom Emotion automatically intercepts with the **Brace Action** without using a **Reaction**. When used this way, the **Brace Action** is 5x as powerful as a regular **Brace**. This means that for every 1 CE you choose to spend on a **Brace**, your **Multiplier** increases by 5. If the attack is a Melee Attack originating from a Sorcerer or Shikigami and you fully block all incoming damage, then the attacker takes damage equal to the difference between the damage they would have dealt and the number rolled for your brace.

**Note: Falling Blossom Emotion can only block attacks that are able to be physically interacted with. Esoteric attacks or environmental effects are not affected in this way**
- Ex. Unlimited Voids Sure-Hit Effect, The Attractive Force of Blue, Hakari’s Sure-Hit Effects
- Cost: 0
