***
The Curtain is essential to the function of Jujutsu Society and the most fundamental **Barrier Technique**. It allows sorcerers to conceal their activity from non-sorcerers as well as draw out troublesome curses. Curtains are cheap, block non sorcerers from perceiving the space within, and create a nighttime environment that draws out curses. In addition, all Jujutsu is enhanced within a Curtain.
- Cost: 2 CE
- Environmental Boost:
    - Curses are 25% more likely to be encountered
	- Sorcerers gain a 10% boost to CE Output while within a curtain

**Learning Cost:**
- 30 MP
- 1 day of downtime
