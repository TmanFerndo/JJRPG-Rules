“The Domain for The Weak” which was born in the Heian Era is a powerful **Anti-Domain Technique** that expands a small **Domain** without a **Cursed Technique** around the user. This "Simple" Domain clashes with the opponents Domain and neutralizes its **sure-hit effect**. When you cast Simple Domain, you are locked into place as part of the **Binding Vow** required to cast it. If you move from your position, your Simple Domain will break. This limitation is removed after you have reached **level 20**, however the Simple Domain will not move from the original casting location. Simple Domain will clash with any **barrier** for 2 rounds before collapsing. In addition, your **Barrier Investment** towards **Domains** strengthens your Simple Domain. For every 50 MP you have invested in **Barrier Investment**, your Simple Domain can last for one round longer up to a maximum of 6 rounds. However, if the domain you are clashing against is at least 300 MP above your Simple Domain, then your Simple Domain will collapse after 1 round regardless. The starting radius of a Simple Domain is 2 meters, but this range increases by 1 meter for every 50 MP invested in **Barrier Investment** up to a maximum of 6m.
