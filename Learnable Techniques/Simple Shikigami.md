Simple Shikigami are Cursed Energy Constructs that can be controlled by the user. They typically require Talismans and Handsigns to summon. To summon a simple Shikigami, you must use a Quick Action. To command the Shikigami you must use an Action and it takes its turn immediately after yours. You can dismiss the Shikigami as an Action to regain 25% of the Cursed Energy spent (rounded down) The Shikigami has the following stats:
- 4 hp for every 2 points of CE expended
- 1 stat point of your choice for every 10 CE you expend

For example: if you spend 10 CE, your Shikigami would have 20 HP and a +1 to any stat

**Learning Cost:**
- 50 MP
- 1 day of downtime
