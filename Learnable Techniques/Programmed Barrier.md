The most advanced **barrier technique**—other than **Domain Expansion** of course—achievable by a regular sorcerer. A Programmed Barrier is a barrier that has been given a set of rules or parameters to govern its function. These rules are typically enforced by **Binding Vows**, as such the rules you impose upon a Programmed Barrier are limited by the restrictions on your **Binding Vow** skill. The rules can either govern the parameters of the barrier itself or enforce rules upon those within it.
- 10 CE + (5 CE/Binding Vow)

**Learning Cost:**
- 400 MP-
- 3 days of downtime
- Must be taught
- Requires knowledge of Simple Domain
