***
A Programmed Shikigami is a Cursed Energy Construct that is made to follow certain rules. The primary benefit of a Programmed Shikigami is that it can function independently from the user based on simple commands. Programmed Shikigami can also be imbued with a Cursed Technique to grant the Shikigami limited power.
- Cost: same as Simple Shikigami

**Learning Cost:**
 - 500 MP
- 3 days of downtime
- Must be taught
- Requires knowledge of Simple Shikigami
