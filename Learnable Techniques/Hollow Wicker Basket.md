Hollow Wicker Basket is an ancient **Anti-Domain Technique** from the Heian Era. Unlike **Simple Domain**, Hollow Wicker Basket requires a **hand sign** where both hands are clasped together with the fingers interlocked. As long as you maintain this **hand sign**, Hollow Wicker Basket cannot be broken and the barrier of the opposing Domain is neutralized which protects you from the **Sure-hit effect** of the domain.
- Cost: 10 CE

**Learning Cost:**
- 500 MP
- 3 days of downtime
