Simple Barrier is the first Barrier Technique you can learn. It does exactly what it sounds like: erect a protective barrier. This barrier starts off cheap, weak, and short ranged but can be strengthened with additional Cursed Energy.
- Cost: 5 CE
- HP: 25
- Radius: 6 meters centered on yourself

for every 5 CE you spend on a Simple Barrier, you can choose to either:
- Strengthen it by 10 HP
- Boost the radius by 1 meter

**Learning Cost:**
- 25 MP
- 1 day of downtime
