Domain Amplification is a more refined version of Simple Domain. Instead of fully expanding an unimbued barrier, Domain Amplification instead envelopes the user in a very thin domain that remains unimbued with a Cursed Technique. This allows an opposing Cursed Technique to flow into the barrier and be neutralized. This can be used to neutralize the Sure-Hit of a Domain as well as weaken any Cursed Techniques encountered in this way. While Domain Amplification is active, any Techniques that try to affect the user are weakened. While DA is active, any Braces you use to block attacks that originate from a Cursed Technique have their cost halved (minimum of 1 CE). If the Technique would require the target to make a saving throw, then the target has Advantage on the roll. While Domain Amplification is active, you cannot use your Cursed Technique 
- Cost: 65 CE
	- Upkeep: 10 CE

**Learning Cost:**
- 1000 MP
- 6 days of downtime
- Must be taught
