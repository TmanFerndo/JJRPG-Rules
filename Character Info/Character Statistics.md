# Ability Scores
In Jujutsu RPG, your physical capabilities are determined by your stats. These being, Strength (STR), Durability (DUR), Speed (SPD), and Perception (PER). A player's stats exist on a spectrum from sub-human capabilities to super human capabilities. For the sake of math, stats have been converted into number values that range from -6 (lowest) to +18 (highest). In this case, -6 represents weakest feats, +18 represents the strongest feats, and 0 represents average adult human feats. Without Heavenly Restriction, Special Physiology, or The Six Eyes, it is normally impossible to have permanent stats above +6. However, certain technique abilities may temporarily boost your stats beyond this limit.
# Levels, Health, MP, and Grade
Your strength and skill as a Sorcerer is tracked by a few metrics. The first is your Sorcerer Level. Sorcerer level is a direct measure of your strength that represents the skills and abilities you have gained. As you defeat enemies, complete quests, and roleplay as your character, you will gain experience as a Sorcerer. The other primary metric is your Sorcerer Grade. Grades are an in-universe “rank” system that tracks your progress in the Jujutsu Hierarchy.
### Level
When you first start out, your sorcerer is fresh to the world of Jujutsu. They have no skills and minimal control over their Technique. This is represented by “Level 1”. At level 1, your HP starts at 100, your CE Pool starts at 100, and your output starts at 5x. As you level up, you gain improvements to your HP, CE Pool, and Output:
- +1 to Output every level up
- 20 + DUR HP per level up
- +20 CE to CE Pool every level up
- +1 stat point into any stat every 3 levels
### MP
In order to level up, you must acquire Mastery Points or MP. MP is typically awarded for the completion of quests and defeating enemies but can also be awarded for good roleplay moments or other non-combat reasons. Typically speaking, the more difficult the challenge of a quest, then the more MP that will be rewarded. The starting amount of MP required to level up is 50 and each subsequent level up adds 50 MP to the required amount to level up.
- On occasion, a sorcerer may be granted the ability to learn Barrier Techniques. When this happens, a sorcerer may choose to invest some of their MP into learning Barrier Techniques. They cannot invest enough MP to de-level themselves but can reduce their MP to 0. The amount of MP they have invested in this way is represented by “Barrier Investment” and will affect things like Domain Refinement and what Barrier Techniques they can learn.
	- A sorcerer cannot invest in Barrier Techniques whenever they want. When they can will be determined by the DM
### Health
As mentioned, your starting HP is 100. This represents your physical endurance in a fight as well as how close you are to death. When your HP reaches zero, you fall unconscious and must make Death Saving Throws. Death Saving Throws are a straight d10 roll requiring a roll of 6 or higher in order to succeed. Death Saving Throws are a best of five system. This means you need at least three successes in order to survive but you can roll up to five Death Saving Throws in the event of a tie. 

If you roll a majority success, your sorcerer remains unconscious. If you roll a majority failure, your sorcerer succumbs to their wounds and dies. After the third roll, if it becomes impossible for you to succeed or fail, you no longer have to make Death Saving Throws and the outcome is determined early.

When leveling up, you gain HP equal to your durability plus 20.
- HP = DUR + 20

Additional HP can be rewarded for quest completion.
### Grade
In the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, both Sorcerers and Curses are classified by Grade. They range from 4-1 and include a category called Special Grade that is reserved for sorcerers and threats too strong to be classified by the Grading system. In this rule book, there is an additional grade for threats even stronger than Special Grade called Disaster Grade. This is reserved for threats as strong or stronger than the Disaster Curses like Jogo and Mahito.

Your Grade as a sorcerer represents your perceived strength in Jujutsu Society. It does not follow your level, but there are certain level requirements to each grade. Your sorcerer will start at Grade 4 and slowly work their way up in the totem pole. In order to rank up a Grade, a sorcerer must complete a Rank Up quest which is typically a harder than usual mission accompanied by another sorcerer to validate the completion. Increasing your Grade can provide various benefits largely having to do with privileges in Jujutsu Society as well as access to harder missions.
