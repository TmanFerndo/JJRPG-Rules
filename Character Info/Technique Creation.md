# Technique Creation
When writing your Cursed Technique, there are a lot of factors that you need to keep in mind. The style of JJK’s setting, the background of your Sorcerer, and the power scaling of the world all have an effect on your technique. While the many facets of Jujutsu may seem intimidating, they can be broken down into simpler aspects to help you write your technique.
# What Is A Cursed Technique?
In Jujutsu Kaisen, Cursed Techniques are the “Super Powers” that Sorcerers use to combat Curses. They use **Cursed Energy** to generate supernatural effects like levitation, invisible slashes, and teleportation.

Cursed Techniques come in all manner of shapes and sizes and there's really no limit to the type of Cursed Technique you can create. For examples of Cursed Techniques, the Anime and Manga are full of diverse powers. These are listed on the Jujutsu Kaisen Fan Wiki which goes into detail on each cannon technique.

You do not have to use a cannon Cursed Technique. In fact, you are encouraged to write your own. When writing your Cursed Technique, you need to abide by certain rules. These rules don't limit what kind of Cursed Technique you can have, but they do limit what you can do with it.
### Cursed Technique Lapse
The **Lapse** of your Technique is the basic straight forward application of your Technique. For example, if your Technique is Fire Manipulation, then your **Lapse** is the Manipulation of Fire. When writing a **Lapse**, try not to make it a “Grab Bag”. This means that your **Lapse** encompasses multiple different kinds of abilities. If your Technique is Fire Manipulation, it shouldn't give you anything else other than the direct manipulation of fire.

Example - Satoru Gojo, **Cursed Technique Lapse: Blue**
- Satoru Gojo’s **Limitless Technique** is centered around the concept of Infinity. As such, his **Lapse: Blue** creates a point of infinite attraction. This point sucks things closer to it and allows Gojo to manipulate objects with a sort of telekinesis.
### Cursed Technique Reversal
The standard **Lapse** of a Technique is fueled by **Cursed Energy**. When **Reverse Cursed Energy** is pushed through a technique, the opposite effect of the **Lapse** is achieved. Not every Technique needs to have a **Reversal** and some Techniques cannot have a **Reversal** because there isn't an “opposite effect”. For example, Fire Manipulation wouldn't have a **Reversal** because there is no opposite to Manipulating Fire. When writing a good **Reversal**, try to think about what the exact opposite concept of your **Lapse** is and keep in mind that not all techniques should have a **Reversal.**

Example - Satoru Gojo, **Cursed Technique Reversal: Red**
- Satoru Gojo’s **Lapse: Blue** creates an attractive force, so his **Reversal: Red** creates a repulsive force. The **Reversal** is far more destructive than the **Lapse** but it cannot be used for the same telekinetic purposes.
### Cursed Technique Extension
An **Extension** is a way of applying your Cursed Technique in a different way than its standard application. It doesn't change the rules or effects, but rather adjusts the targets or uses said effects to produce a different result. Manipulation style techniques tend to have a lot of **Extensions**. For Fire Manipulation, a good **Extension** would be using the fire to propel oneself forward like a rocket. **Extensions** don't have to apply to just your **Lapse**, but can also apply to your **Reversal** as well. When writing **Extensions**, think about creative ways to use your Cursed Technique that may not be explicitly outlined in your **Lapse** or **Reversal**. Do try to limit yourself to no more than 3 **Extensions** unless you are using a Manipulation type technique. While this is not a hard limit, it does help prevent Techniques from becoming “Grab Bags” as previously mentioned.

Example - Choso, **Cursed Technique Extension: Flowing Red Scale**
- The standard application of Blood Manipulation uses the user's blood as a weapon. **Flowing Red Scale** instead manipulates the user's blood within their body to increase blood flow and oxygen saturation. This has the effect of increasing their physical stats by a small amount
### Maximum Technique
The **Maximum** of a Technique is essentially the idea of “pushing the technique to its limit”. This means that the basic concept of the lapse is amplified to its absolute extreme for a very powerful effect. This concept does overlap with **Domain Expansion** somewhat but is differentiated in its use. **Maximum Techniques** are treated like Super Moves that can cause drastic tempo shifts in a fight where **Domain Expansion** is typically an instant win condition. Like **Reversals**, not every technique needs a **Maximum Technique**. As a general rule, if you cannot think of a **Maximum** that doesn't overlap with your **Domain Expansion**, you do not need to make a **Maximum Technique**.

Example - Jogo, **Maximum Technique: Meteor**
- Jogo's Cursed Technique—**Disaster Flames**—allows him to manipulate fire and earth. Therefore his **Maximum Technique** is the Manipulation of Fire and Earth to an extreme degree. **Maximum Meteor** allows Jogo to summon an incredibly large ball of rock and fire to hurl at his enemy for huge damage. This does come at the cost of large **Cursed Energy** expenditure.
### Domain Expansion
**Domain Expansion** is considered to be the pinnacle of Jujutsu. It combines every mechanic of the power system including **Cursed Energy**, **Barrier Techniques**, and **Binding Vows**. A **Domain Expansion** creates an arena with terrain and landscape unique to the user. The Domain is then imbued with the user's **Cursed Technique Lapse** which becomes an Auto-Hit attack that targets anyone and anything caught within the Domain. When writing your Domain, the primary thing to think about is your **Sure-Hit Effect**. The **Sure-Hit Effect** is derived from your **Lapse**, but it does not need to have an exactly identical effect. Most domains have a Lethal **Sure-Hit** that takes the form of an attack, but some Domains instead enforce rules onto the targets caught within it. Your **Sure-Hit** does not need to be damage based. Domains are as diverse as sorcerers themselves so feel free to get creative. In addition, Domains are meant to be the ultimate power of Jujutsu so do not be afraid to give your domains powerful effects. Just be sure to stick to your **Lapse** as a close template.

Example 1. - Sukuna, **Domain Expansion: Malevolent Shrine**
- Sukuna's Cursed Technique allows him to fire off invisible Cuts and Slashes. So, his Domain automatically applies these slashes to all targets throughout the Domain.

Example 2. - Hakari, **Domain Expansion: Restless Gambler**
- Instead of having a lethal **Sure-Hit**, Hakari's Domain forcefully injects the targets with information on the Domain's rules. In addition, the Domain allows Hakari to start gambling, and if he wins, he gains immortality and infinite Cursed Energy for 4 minutes and 11 seconds.
# Technique Costs
In order to balance techniques, costs will be assigned to the different aspects of a Cursed Technique. As a general rule, the basic **Lapse** of a Technique will cost between 3-5 CE but this value can go up or down based on the strength of the technique. 

**Reversals** will be roughly double the CE value of the **Lapse** but this value can also go up or down based on its strength. **Extensions** are applied on a case by case basis. 

**Maximum Techniques** will cost around 125 CE + 20% of your Max CE and **Domain Expansion** will cost 250 CE + 40% of your Max CE.
# Technique Skill Tree
When you first start out, your sorcerer will not have their full Cursed Technique unlocked. Most sorcerers will start with just their basic **Lapse** and unlock more aspects of their technique as they level up. What they unlock can include the following:
- An **Extension**
- **Reversal**
- **Maximum Technique**

A sorcerer unlocks more of their technique every 5 levels and should have unlocked their full Cursed Technique by level 20. Techniques with more applications will need to have more unlocks per technique upgrade, however, this also means that each individual **extension** will be weaker. Technique upgrades can also include improvements on technique efficiency that reduce cost, upgrade power, or reduce the activation cost of things like **Chants** and **Hand Signs**. You will work with your DM to determine your Skill Tree.
# Technique Damage
If your technique would deal damage, use the Attack Rolls and Damage sections as a guideline. As a general rule, the strength of your Technique determines the type of damage dice you use.
- Weak Attack: 1d4 + 2
- Medium Attack: 1d6 + 3
- Strong Attack: 1d8 + 4
- Special Grade Attack: 1d10 + 5
- Divine Attack: 1d12 + 6

If your technique creates an AoE that damages, use the following chart.
- Weak Attack: 1d4 - 1 (Minimum of 1)
- Medium Attack: 1d4 + 2
- Strong Attack: 1d6 + 3
- Special Grade Attack: 1d8 + 4
- Divine Attack: 1d10 +5
