# Reaching 0 Hit Points
When your character is reduced to 0 HP, they are not dead outright. Instead, they are simply on the verge of death. When your character is reduced to 0 HP, they are unconscious and you roll Death Saves at the start of your turn.
# Death Saves
When you roll a Death Save, you roll a d10 to determine success or failure. You add no bonuses to the roll and cannot benefit from any buffs or debuffs. On a roll of 6 or higher, the roll is counted as a success. On a 5 or lower, the roll is counted as a failure.

Death Saves are a best of 5 system. This means that if you ever have a number of successes or failures totaling 3 or more, the outcome is decided. On a majority success, your character is stable but unconscious and is no longer dying. On a majority failure, your character is dead.
